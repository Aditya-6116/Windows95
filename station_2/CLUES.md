## Overview
Across the vintage Windows 95 interface, 11 hidden characters are embedded within intuitive, top-level menus, sub-menus, desktop icons, and top-level file viewers. 

Participants can easily spot and collect all 11 characters within **5 to 10 minutes** simply by exploring the Desktop, clicking the Start Menu and its sub-menus, and opening standard windows (Notepad and File Explorer).

Clicking on any anomalous character automatically records it into the **Evidence Collected** dossier.

- **`R`** (`clue-10`): In **Notepad**, the top menu bar has `Help (R)`.
- **`E`** (`clue-11`): In **File Explorer**, the top menu bar has `View (E)`.
